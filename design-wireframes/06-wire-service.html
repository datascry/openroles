Direction 6 · Wire Service

AP / Reuters wire-service aesthetic. Each job listing is a bulletin: condensed display sans for the headline, a serif lede, a dateline showing city and source ATS, and a tabular set of facts. Higher density than Quiet Editorial — closer to a printed bulletin than a magazine — but still typographically driven, no cards, no shadows. Flagging "★" and "live" use a single wire-red accent. Reads like the morning roundup, not a feed.
